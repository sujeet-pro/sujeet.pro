## Topology simulator

Pick a level to see the components that stage earns, and switch between generic building blocks and their AWS services. Select any component, then inject a 10× slowdown or an outage to see its blast radius and the fallback path. "Show changes" overlays what the level added, changed or removed versus the one before.

## Cost economics

The table follows the level picked above. Unit prices are AWS `us-east-1` list prices as of 2026-11-23, linked under the table; usage volumes (traffic, task sizes, miss rates, log sizes) are modelled and marked ASSUMPTION. The enterprise range (25–40% off list) is also an ASSUMPTION: AWS publishes no discount schedule.
